Landau's 4 Problems

4 problems · 1912

Four problems about primes listed by Edmund Landau in 1912, easy to state and still unsolved.

1. Goldbach's conjecture

For every even integer $n > 2$, there are primes $p$ and $q$ with $n = p + q$.

2. Twin prime conjecture

There are infinitely many primes $p$ such that $p + 2$ is also prime.

3. Legendre's conjecture

For every positive integer $n$, there is a prime $p$ with $n^2 < p < (n+1)^2$.

4. Infinitely many primes of the form $n^2 + 1$

There are infinitely many positive integers $n$ such that $n^2 + 1$ is prime.
