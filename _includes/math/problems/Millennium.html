Millennium Prize Problems

7 problems

Seven problems chosen by the Clay Mathematics Institute in 2000, each carrying a one-million-dollar prize; only the Poincaré conjecture has been solved.

1. Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture

2. Hodge conjecture

3. Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness

4. P versus NP

5. Poincaré conjecture

6. Riemann hypothesis

7. Yang–Mills existence and mass gap
