deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-Prover-V2 Open-source language model for formal theorem proving in Lean 4, trained with reinforcement learning on subgoal decomposition.

  • Cold-start data comes from recursive proof search: DeepSeek-V3 decomposes a theorem into subgoals and a 7B prover solves each one.
  • DeepSeek-Prover-V2-671B reaches an 88.9% pass ratio on MiniF2F-test and solves 49 of 658 PutnamBench problems.
