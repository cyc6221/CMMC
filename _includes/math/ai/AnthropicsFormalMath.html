3sum-apsp

notes {% include note-link.html href="/articles/Math/ai/anthropic/3sum-apsp/" label="3sum-apsp" %}

The first polynomial improvements over the textbook $O(n^2)$ 3SUM and $O(n^3)$ APSP algorithms on a word RAM, followed by a simplified proof and smaller exponents.

  • Alman and Vassilevska Williams: $O(n^{1.9992})$ for 3SUM and $O(n^{2.99942})$ for APSP, via triangles in sparse lopsided graphs. arXiv:2610.06783
  • Karingula and Lovett: a simplified proof of the polynomial improvements, with weaker exponents. arXiv:2610.15663
  • Majdoddin: improved exponents $n^{1.99450+o(1)}$ for 3SUM and $n^{2.99450+o(1)}$ for APSP. arXiv:2610.17420

eth {% include gh-link.html href="https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/blob/main/eth/paper.pdf" label="paper" aria_label="paper: A 2 to the o(n) Time Algorithm for 3-SAT on GitHub" %}

notes {% include note-link.html href="/articles/Math/others/exponential-time-hypothesis/" label="Exponential Time Hypothesis" %} {% include note-link.html href="/articles/Math/ai/anthropic/subexponential-3sat/" label="2^o(n) 3-SAT" %}

A deterministic algorithm counts the satisfying assignments of a 3-CNF on $n$ variables in time $2^{O(n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n})}$, which refutes the Exponential Time Hypothesis. The result was discovered by an internal research Claude model.

  • Key idea: the eight-square identity rewrites each pair of crossing equality tests on a grid of nodes, so that merging its rows or its columns cuts the bits on the edges by a constant factor at every level of the recursion.
  • Rederivation: from a short condensed description, Claude Opus 5.5 at high effort rederives that 3-SAT can be decided in $O(2^{\delta n})$ time for every $\delta>0$.
