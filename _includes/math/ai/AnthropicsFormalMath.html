anthropics/formal-math

Machine-checked Lean 4 formalizations published by Anthropic. Each project is a self-contained Lake project following the Palomar submission layout.

  • zeta23: more than two thirds of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line.
  • 3sum-apsp: faster word RAM algorithms for 3SUM, (min,+)-product and APSP.
