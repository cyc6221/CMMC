#109

Integer multiplication below $n\log n$ preprint: deterministic $O(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$ on a multitape Turing machine, disproving the Schönhage–Strassen $n\log n$ optimality conjecture in that model.

  • CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds repo: a community effort to optimize the construction and push $\kappa$ higher. It is conditional on the #109 framework and is not an official OpenAI release.
