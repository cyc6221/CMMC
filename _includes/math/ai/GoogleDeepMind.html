google-deepmind Repositories accompanying DeepMind's work on mathematical and algorithmic discovery.

  • alphaevolve_results
    Colab notebook with AlphaEvolve's mathematical discoveries and the code verifying them.
  • alphaevolve_repository_of_problems
    67 mathematical problems with prompts, verification code and evolved programs from Mathematical exploration and discovery at scale.
  • formal-conjectures
    Lean formalizations of the statements of open conjectures, useful as a benchmark for automated theorem provers.
  • funsearch
    Functions and sets discovered by FunSearch (cap sets, admissible sets, bin packing, and more) and the search pipeline.
  • alphadev
    Pseudocode of the AlphaDev agent and the discovered assembly programs for small sorting networks.
